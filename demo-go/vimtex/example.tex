\section{Navigation}
For longer documents, move the mouse over the right-hand preview and use the
wheel to move through source blocks. Saving preserves the approximate position.
Use \texttt{:VimtexPreview} to preview around Vim's current line, or
\texttt{:VimtexScroll 3} to advance three blocks. Use \texttt{:q} to exit.
